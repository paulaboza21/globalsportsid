Global Sports ID Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Global Sports ID ("we", "our", "us") provides a platform for players and coaches to create profiles, discover opportunities, communicate, and share sports-related information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we store it, and the choices available to users.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Password and authentication information handled through Supabase Auth
  • Role information such as player or coach
  • Sport selection
  • Team, city, country, nationality, age, position, gender, biography, stats, and highlights link
  • Profile photo, if uploaded
  • Messages, contact requests, trials, and offers created through the app
  • Technical information needed to operate the app, such as session and authentication data

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Display player and coach profiles
  • Match users within the same sport
  • Enable messaging, contact requests, trials, and offers
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and enforce platform rules
  • Improve and support the app

4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared:

  • With other users, where profile and communication features require it
  • With service providers that help us operate the app, including hosting, authentication, and database infrastructure
  • If required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights and safety

5. User Visibility

Because Global Sports ID is a networking platform:

  • Profile information may be visible to other authenticated users within the same sport
  • Messages and requests are only intended to be visible to the relevant participants
  • Trials and offers may be visible to authenticated users within the same sport

6. Data Storage and Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect user information, including secure authentication, access controls, database security policies, and restricted data access based on role and sport.

No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

8. User Rights

Depending on where a user lives, they may have rights to access their data, correct their data, request deletion of their data, or object to certain uses of their data.

9. Children and Age Requirements

Users should only use the platform if they are legally permitted to do so in their jurisdiction. If we learn that personal data was collected improperly, we may remove the related account or information.

10. International Users

If users access the app from outside the country where our systems operate, their information may be processed in other countries where our service providers operate.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: info@globalsportsid.com
